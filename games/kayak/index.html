        Controls
        
    
          
  • ← → paddle strokes. Alternate them on a steady beat, up to about 90 a minute: rhythm is speed, and holding it builds flow, a few percent more over the next 20 seconds. Uneven strokes, two on one side, steering hard and bumping into people all knock you off it
    • 
          
  • Shift or Space (held) to sprint: every stroke pulls harder, and you can go quicker than about 90 a minute. It spends the sprint bar. Use it to catch someone, then rest on their wash: the bar fills there several times faster. Without sprinting, a beat quicker than about 90 gets you nothing extra
    • 
          
  • The start: you begin behind the field. Paddle up into a gap on the line, stop, and wait for the horn. It comes without warning after "Are you ready?" Go early and everyone waits while the line comes up to you
    • 
          
  • A D lean to keep your balance. Standing still is wobbly; speed steadies you
    • 
          
  • Q E rudder. It only bites when you're moving
    • 
          
  • Tuck in right behind a boat, or alongside its back half, to ride its wash: you go further for less effort and your sprint fills up, but the boat rocks more
    • 
          
  • Race distances and times are shown four times longer than you actually paddle, so a 6 km race takes about 7 minutes. On the Reading Thames and the Time Trial Loop the river is true to size and you cover it four times faster
    • 
          
  • Time Trial Loop: boats go one at a time, 30 seconds apart, and your time runs from the start line. Keep right: after the turns, the boats ahead come back the other way. The gap at St Mary's Island takes two boats at most
    • 
          
  • Swans won't get out of your way: paddle into one and you're in. Ducks fly off
    • 
        
  • Other boats use the river too: scullers, eights, cruisers and motorboats. They keep to their right, so keep right and they pass on your left. Hit one, or its oars, and you're in; a motorboat's wash rocks you. Now and then an eight comes up from behind: the cox shouts and an arrow shows which side, so move over the other way
    • 
          
  • Gamepad: LT RT strokes, left stick rudder, right stick lean, hold A to sprint
    • 
          
  • Phone, held sideways: tap the left or right half of the screen to stroke, in time with the rings at the sides. Tap Sprint for a burst, and again to stop. To steer, put a finger down and slide it left or right; the bar under the balance gauge shows your rudder. Turn the phone like a steering wheel to shift your weight: when the boat dips one way, lean the other, as you would in a real kayak. (Tilt needs the page over HTTPS on iPhone; without it, balance is done for you)
    • 
          
  • P or Esc to pause, M to toggle sound
